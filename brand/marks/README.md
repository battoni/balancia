# Marks — exploration log

## Round 1 — 16/11/2026 — rejected

Ten symbols drawn from the contract in [`../SURVEY.md`](../SURVEY.md), each from a different part of
the synthesis. The owner's verdict: **"I'm not liking the logo already."** The whole round was
rejected; nothing here survives as the mark. The logo work moved to outside design AIs (ChatGPT,
Gemini) using [`../DESIGN-AI-BRIEF.md`](../DESIGN-AI-BRIEF.md).

Screenshot: [`round-1-rejected.png`](round-1-rejected.png) · light/dark preview:
[`preview.html`](preview.html) · real-pixel size ladder: [`ladder.html`](ladder.html).

| # | File | Idea | 16px | Why it doesn't work (my read) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | `01-t-account.svg` | Conta T in negative space: mint bar over two teal blocks | holds | Reads as a "layout/dashboard" UI icon |
| 02 | `02-closed-balance.svg` | Two equal pillars on a mint total rule | holds | Reads as a pause button with an underline |
| 03 | `03-meeting-point.svg` | Two halves split by a narrowing V (hidden funnel) | the V closes up | Reads as "M"/"V", heavy |
| 04 | `04-stacked-b.svg` | B from two stacked blocks with windows | windows fill in | Reads as a 6 or an "E"; generic monogram |
| 05 | `05-double-rule.svg` | Block over the accountant's double underline | lines merge | Reads as a hamburger menu / text document |
| 06 | `06-fitted-entry.svg` | Two L-brackets locked into a square | holds | Reads as an "S" or a Tetris piece |
| 07 | `07-in-level.svg` | Spirit level: two blocks around a mint bubble | too small | Too wide: tiny at 16px, reads as a slider |
| 08 | `08-parts-equal-whole.svg` | Two blocks over one wide block: parts = total | holds best | Same layout-icon problem as 01 |
| 09 | `09-balance-point.svg` | Rounded square with a centre notch | holds | Reads as a box or a shopping bag |
| 10 | `10-two-pages.svg` | Two pages leaning out on a mint binding | holds | Reads as a "U" or an open book |

**What the round taught:**
- Literal rectangles go wrong. Every "two equal blocks" mark ended up looking like a generic UI
  glyph (layout, pause, menu) rather than a brand, so the next round needs a form with character, not
  just a correct construction.
- Thick shapes with gaps of at least 40/512 survive 16px. Double lines, windows and narrowing gaps do
  not.
- The palette (petrol teal + mint) and the dark-ground swap (mint mark, pale-mint accent) were not
  part of the complaint.

## Round 2 — 16/11/2026 — direction 01 "Fluxo em Equilíbrio" chosen

The owner ran [`../DESIGN-AI-BRIEF.md`](../DESIGN-AI-BRIEF.md) in an outside design AI, which
returned six directions ([`round-2/reference-6-directions.png`](round-2/reference-6-directions.png)).
The owner picked **01 "Fluxo em Equilíbrio"**: two complementary rounded forms, dark teal over mint,
split by a rising S-shaped gap. Together they read as a soft "B", a cycle that closes in balance.

Recreated by hand as clean SVG (the AI's own SVG code in the image was illegible/garbled):
- [`round-2/balancia-icon.svg`](round-2/balancia-icon.svg) for light grounds (teal `#0e5a5c` over mint `#2fb7a3`).
- [`round-2/balancia-icon-dark.svg`](round-2/balancia-icon-dark.svg) for dark grounds (mint over pale mint `#bfeee5`).
- The gap is a little wider than in the reference (~30–40/512) so it stays open at 16px.

Size ladder ([`round-2/ladder-src/size-ladder.html`](round-2/ladder-src/size-ladder.html)): **passes**,
the two forms stay separate at real 16px. Black and white, and on dark: passes.
Lockup preview: [`round-2/preview.html`](round-2/preview.html).
