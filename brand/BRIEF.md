# Balancia — what it is, and what it is not

**Balancia** is a white-label SaaS that Brazilian accounting firms subscribe to so they can win
clients and charge fair fees. Each firm gets:
- **Its own site** under its brand, with a free impact diagnostic and meeting booking.
- **A back office** where the team works the contacts until they become clients.
- **Fee repricing** for the clients the firm already has, ending in a ready-to-send letter.

> "A plataforma que faz o escritório contábil ganhar clientes novos e cobrar o justo dos que já tem."

| It is | It is not |
| --- | --- |
| The whole package in one place: branded site → diagnostic → funnel → repricing letter | A tax simulator (those are free) |
| A vertical product for accounting firms | A generic marketing CRM |
| A growth tool that outlives the Reforma (the transition ends in 2033) | A "Reforma kit": the Reforma is the launch campaign, not the brand |
| The platform behind the firm; the firm's brand leads with its own clients | A brand the firm's clients are meant to notice (only a discreet "powered by Balancia") |
| Professional, accessible premium (R$ 490–1.490/month) | A cheap or free tool |

**Who buys it:** the owner or partner of a small-to-mid firm (10–300 clients), aged 35–55,
comfortable with software but not technical. They care about revenue first and want it to work out
of the box.

**Where the Balancia brand appears:**
- Its own site (the root domain).
- The sales pitch and proposals.
- The platform console.
- The login and loading screens.
- The browser tab.
- A one-colour "powered by" line in tenant footers and e-mails.

**Never in the brand:** the commercial partner, his firm and Franco Contabilidade (the first
tenant) never appear in the Balancia brand.
