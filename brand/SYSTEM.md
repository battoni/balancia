# Balancia — SYSTEM: what to do before you make anything

The contract for anyone (person or agent) creating a screen, page, e-mail, slide or post for
**Balancia's own surfaces**: the root site, the platform console, login, pitch and proposals.

A firm tenant's screens follow the firm's brand. Only the "powered by" signature is Balancia's.

Generic UI rules live in `.claude/rules/celer-*` and the codelumen handbook. This file does not
repeat them; it only says what is specific to Balancia.

## 1. Before you create anything

Read, in this order:
1. This file.
2. [`tokens.md`](tokens.md).
3. [`BRAND.md`](BRAND.md), if you touch the logo.

Then open [`design-system/index.html`](design-system/index.html) to see everything rendered.

| What | Source of truth |
| --- | --- |
| Colour, type | `app.rnc/src/styles/theme/themes/balancia.css` |
| Radius, shadow, motion, state colours | The bootstrap theme in `app.rnc/src/styles/` |
| Logo files | `logo/`, generated by `build.py` from `logo/balancia-icon.svg` |
| Every brand decision and its reason | [`SURVEY.md`](SURVEY.md) |

## 2. Colour

**Use roles, never values:**
- **Surfaces:** `canvas`, `panel`, `panel-muted`.
- **Text:** `heading`, `body`, `muted`. `subtle` is only for placeholders, disabled text and large text.
- **Lines:** `line`, `line-strong`.
- **Action:** `primary-500`, with hover `600` and pressed `700`.
- **On dark:** links and active items use `primary-300` or `primary-400`.
- **States:** the bootstrap's `success`, `info`, `warn` and `danger`.

**Forbidden:**
- **Loose values:** a hex value written in a component, or Tailwind's or PrimeVue's default palette.
- **Mint as text on light:** it measures 2.49:1.
- **Gradients:** never, anywhere.
- **Red as a brand colour:** red only ever means an error.
- **Blue with yellow, or orange as the main colour.**

**Mark-only colours:** `--brand-mint` on a dark surface and `--brand-pale-mint` are reserved for the logo.

## 3. Type

- **Manrope** for everything, with Sometype Mono for codes and numeric tables.
- Use the scale in `tokens.md`. **Nothing below 12px.**
- Headings are 700 (display 800), labels 500, body 400.
- The number that matters is set in **700 with `tabular-nums`**.

## 4. Space, radius, relief

These are inherited from the bootstrap.
- **Cards and panels:** `radius-lg` (12px).
- **Inputs and buttons:** `radius-md` (8px).
- **Tags:** `radius-sm` (5px).
- **Relief:** one level of shadow (`shadow-sm`) for cards, `shadow-lg` only for overlays. Never glow.

## 5. The mark

- **Full lockup** (`balancia-logo*.svg`): headers, login, site, slides. Use it whenever there is width.
- **Icon alone** (`balancia-icon*.svg`): favicon, square avatars, the "powered by" signature, tight spaces.
- **Clear space:** 0.25 × icon height on every side.
- **Minimum size:** lockup 20px tall, icon 16px.
- **Match the background:** `-dark` on dark, the default on light, `-white` on teal or on a photo with a scrim, `-mono` for one-colour print.
- **Never:** recolour, stretch, rotate, add a stroke or shadow, change the gap, or retype the wordmark as live text.
- **"Powered by Balancia" in tenant footers and e-mails:** icon at 16px plus the text "powered by Balancia" in `muted`. One colour, never larger.

## 6. Voice

- **Address the reader as "você".** Be direct and calm, with short sentences that **lead with the number**.
- **No humour and no emoji in the product.**
- **Glossary:**
  - **escritório:** never "empresa" or "tenant".
  - **contato:** on screen. "Lead" stays a technical word.
  - **reajuste de honorários**
  - **diagnóstico:** never "simulação".
- **Never in the brand layer:** "grátis", "simulador", "feche mais", "venda mais", or the Reforma as a slogan.

| Like this | Not like this |
| --- | --- |
| Não conseguimos salvar o reajuste. Seus dados estão intactos; tente de novo em instantes. | Erro 500: falha ao processar a requisição. |
| Carta pronta: 42 clientes, R$ 18.300 a mais por ano. | Prontinho! 🎉 Sua carta foi gerada com sucesso! |
| 3 contatos novos esta semana. Marina, da Padaria Alvorada, marcou reunião para quinta. | Bora vender mais! Seu funil está bombando 🚀 |

## 7. Imagery

- **Real product screenshots with real numbers.** Frame them on `primary-50` with a 12px radius. No tilt, no 3D, no glow.
- **Photos only of real customers:** firm owners at their desk, in natural light.
- **Never:** stock photos, smiling accountants with calculators, flag colours, Reforma imagery or anything that looks like government.

## 8. Icons and motion

**Icons:**
- **Library:** Font Awesome 7. Use **Regular** by default and **Solid only for the active state**.
- **PrimeIcons:** only inside PrimeVue components.
- **No label:** an icon goes without a label only for close, search and menu, and always with an `aria-label`.

**Motion:**
- **Speed:** `--motion-quick` (180ms) with an ease-out curve for hover, press, panels and toasts. Use `--motion-calm` (320ms) for larger entrances.
- **Loading:** on the loading screen, the logo's two forms settle level.
- **Reduced motion:** under `prefers-reduced-motion`, nothing moves and everything still works.

## 9. Components to reuse before you build new ones

These are on the rnc-4 branch.
- `APlatformLogo`: Balancia's mark (lockup or icon, light/dark/white). `MFirmLogo`, `MNavbarBrand` and `ThePublicLayout` show it while the host firm is the platform firm; `ALogo` stays the firm's mark.
- `MPoweredBy`: the "powered by Balancia" signature under a tenant's pages (the e-mail layout carries its own).
- `brandPalette` / `useBrandTheme`: apply a palette at runtime. Balancia's own palette is the platform firm's.
- The bootstrap's `ABrandMark`, `ABrandPattern` and `ABrandSignature` are **Battoni Dev's** mark, not Balancia's. Never use them as Balancia's logo.

## 10. Delivery checklist

Before you call a piece done:
- [ ] Every text pair is measured: 4.5:1, or 3:1 for icons and large text. Check light and dark.
- [ ] Only tokens: no loose hex, no framework default palette.
- [ ] Manrope throughout, nothing under 12px, figures in tabular numbers.
- [ ] The logo comes from `logo/`, at its minimum size or larger, with its clear space, in the version that matches the background.
- [ ] Voice: "você", number first, no humour, no emoji, glossary words only.
- [ ] Images are real screenshots or real customers.
- [ ] It works with `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- [ ] Nothing presents the Reforma as the brand, says "grátis" or looks like government.
